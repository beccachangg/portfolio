how i work

research. create. measure.

the eecs side of my marketing — every post has a reason behind it.

before i post, i look at the landscape. my python seo tool scrapes 10+ competitor pages and benchmarks word count, readability, keyword density and heading structure. tf-idf surfaces the keyword gaps worth owning, and google trends adds 12 months of interest data and rising queries.
